# Changelog

## 0.1.0 POC — 2026-09-05

First playable foundation of Last Metro.

- Authored four-area station: platform, ticket hall, maintenance corridor, Control.
- First-person mouse look, walking, sprinting, crouching, collision and flashlight.
- Recoverable two-fuse power puzzle with retained clues, correct/wrong routing feedback, service access, dispatch and a train-departure completion screen.
- Pause, focus handling, keyboard-accessible menus, journal, local milestone saves and session fallback when storage is blocked.
- Original procedural station assets and audio, subtitled announcements, optional sound captions, accessibility and quality settings.
- Pinned toolchain, strict TypeScript, state/browser tests, CI configuration, production build, development roadmap, and QA ledger.

This release is a local proof of concept. The full access/evidence puzzles, active enemy, countdown, hiding/distraction mechanics, spatial/recorded audio, branching endings, and production cross-browser/performance validation remain on the roadmap.
