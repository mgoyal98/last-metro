# Changelog

## 0.2.0 Alpha — 2026-09-05

Complete escape loop, extending the playable foundation.

- Staff access puzzle with shift record, locker assignment, stored PA transcript and a physical Control gate.
- Departure evidence across the old route map, timestamped archive and live service board, with a conflicting PA transcript retained in the journal.
- Recoverable three-step departure sequence and two distinct boarding points leading to Departure and Loop endings.
- An 18-minute departure window after orientation, paused in all menus and on focus loss, with fair expiry recovery.
- Version 2 checkpoints and automatic POC-save migration that preserves discoveries and power without bypassing new puzzles.
- Live station displays, expanded objectives/hints, keypad, evidence/sequence panels and ending screens.
- Expanded unit and browser coverage for both endings, wrong inputs, continuous navigation, clock suspension, migration and recovery.

Active enemy AI, hiding/distractions, recorded voices, final atmosphere, Safari validation and hardware performance tuning remain later milestones.

## 0.1.0 POC — 2026-09-05

First playable foundation of Last Metro.

- Authored four-area station: platform, ticket hall, maintenance corridor, Control.
- First-person mouse look, walking, sprinting, crouching, collision and flashlight.
- Recoverable two-fuse power puzzle with retained clues, correct/wrong routing feedback, service access, dispatch and a train-departure completion screen.
- Pause, focus handling, keyboard-accessible menus, journal, local milestone saves and session fallback when storage is blocked.
- Original procedural station assets and audio, subtitled announcements, optional sound captions, accessibility and quality settings.
- Pinned toolchain, strict TypeScript, state/browser tests, CI configuration, production build, development roadmap, and QA ledger.

This release is a local proof of concept. The full access/evidence puzzles, active enemy, countdown, hiding/distraction mechanics, spatial/recorded audio, branching endings, and production cross-browser/performance validation remain on the roadmap.
